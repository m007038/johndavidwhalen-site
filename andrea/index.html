Andrea

A WhatsApp assistant for property enquiries in Lisbon — answering questions, understanding what someone is looking for, and connecting them with the advisory team.

What it does

Andrea responds to enquiries about buying and renting property in Lisbon. She can talk through neighbourhoods, price ranges, residency routes such as the D7 and Digital Nomad visas, and what daily life is actually like in different parts of the city. When an enquiry is ready for a real conversation, she passes it to the advisory team.

How it works

  • Enquiries arrive through WhatsApp
  • Andrea answers and gathers the details needed to help properly
  • Qualified enquiries are recorded and the advisory team is notified
  • A member of the team follows up directly, usually within 24 hours
Andrea is an AI assistant, not a person. Anything she says about prices, taxes, or visas is general guidance to help orient an enquiry — always confirm specifics with a qualified professional. Sending /reset clears your conversation history at any time.
